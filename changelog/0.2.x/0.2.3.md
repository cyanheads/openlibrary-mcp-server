# 0.2.3 — 2026-07-26

## Fixed

- **Empty-title and empty-name headings no longer render as a bare heading marker.** Open Library returns `null` for records with no title or name; the service coalesces that to `''`, and `format()` interpolated it straight into a markdown heading (`### `, nothing after it). New `src/mcp-server/tools/heading-placeholders.ts` exports `NO_TITLE` and `NO_NAME`, applied at all eight affected render sites: the six title headings in `openlibrary_get_work`, `openlibrary_get_author_works`, `openlibrary_get_edition`, `openlibrary_get_editions`, `openlibrary_get_subject`, and `openlibrary_search_books`, plus the two name headings in `openlibrary_get_author` and `openlibrary_search_authors`. `structuredContent` is unchanged (still `''`). ([#28](https://github.com/cyanheads/openlibrary-mcp-server/issues/28))
